# Outsourcing and lending service provider policy (SAMPLE)

## 1. Accountability
1.1 Outsourcing a function does not transfer the bank's responsibility for compliance and customer outcomes.

## 2. Due diligence
2.1 Service providers, including lending service providers and digital lending apps, are assessed for capability, financial soundness, security and conduct before appointment.

## 3. Contracts
3.1 Contracts include data-protection obligations, audit and inspection rights for the bank and the regulator, and termination rights.
3.2 Service providers may collect only the customer data needed for their function, with the customer's consent.

## 4. Customer disclosure
4.1 The customer is told which lending service providers act for the bank, and the names of recovery agents before they make contact.

## 5. Oversight
5.1 Material service providers are reviewed annually. Complaints about a provider are tracked and reported to senior management.
