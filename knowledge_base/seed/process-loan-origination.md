# Loan origination and servicing process

## 1. Lead and application intake
The applicant is acquired through a branch, the lender's app, or a lending service provider. The applicant selects a product, gives consent for data use and credit checks, and submits personal, employment and income details. Partially completed applications can be saved and resumed.

## 2. Identity verification and KYC
The lender verifies identity and address using officially valid documents, CKYC retrieval, or video-based identification. Sanctions and PEP screening run at this stage. A customer risk rating is assigned.

## 3. Document collection
Income proof, bank statements, and property or collateral documents are collected. Documents are checked for completeness and authenticity. Missing items are requested from the applicant with a clear list.

## 4. Credit assessment and underwriting
The lender pulls a credit report from a credit information company, computes affordability (DTI or FOIR), and applies the credit policy's eligibility rules and score cut-offs. Scoring models produce a recommendation. Cases outside policy are referred to a credit officer with the authority level for the amount.

## 5. Decision and communication
The decision is sanction, conditional sanction, or rejection. A rejected applicant is told the principal reasons. A sanctioned applicant receives the Key Fact Statement and the loan agreement before signing.

## 6. Agreement and disbursement
The borrower accepts the terms (digitally or physically). Pre-disbursement conditions are verified. Funds are disbursed directly to the borrower's bank account or a designated account, not through third-party pass-through accounts. Disbursement is reconciled with the core banking system.

## 7. Servicing and collections
EMIs are collected by mandate. Statements, interest certificates and payoff quotes are provided on request. Overdue accounts are tracked by days past due. Collection and recovery follow fair-practices rules, and recovery agents are disclosed to the borrower.

## 8. Closure
On full repayment the loan is closed, collateral documents are returned and liens released within the agreed time, and credit information companies are updated.

## 9. Cross-cutting controls
Every stage records an audit trail (who, what, when, before and after values). Overrides need maker-checker approval and a reason code. Data retention follows the retention policy and regulatory minimums. Grievances can be raised at any stage and are tracked to resolution.
