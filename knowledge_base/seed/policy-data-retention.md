# Records and data retention policy (SAMPLE)

## 1. Principles
1.1 Records are kept for the longer of the period required by law or regulation and the period in this schedule, then securely deleted or anonymised.
1.2 Personal data is not kept longer than needed for the purpose it was collected for, unless the law requires it.

## 2. Retention schedule
2.1 Customer identification and KYC records: 5 years after the relationship ends, or longer if regulation requires.
2.2 Transaction records: 5 years from the date of the transaction.
2.3 Loan files, including application, decision evidence and agreement: 8 years after loan closure.
2.4 Rejected loan applications: 2 years from the decision date.
2.5 Audit logs of critical systems: 5 years.
2.6 Call recordings and video KYC recordings: as required by the applicable direction, and at least 5 years.

## 3. Legal hold
3.1 Records under investigation, litigation or regulatory enquiry are placed on hold and not deleted until the hold is released.

## 4. Deletion
4.1 Deletion is logged with the record class, date and approver. Backups expire on their own rotation schedule.
