Workshop notes: credit operations (Bank A)
Facilitator: business analyst. Participants: credit operations, credit officers.

- Credit officers need a work queue of referred applications. Each referred application must be decided within 4 working hours.
- Salary slips and bank statements are read by OCR; when the extracted income differs from the declared income, the case goes to manual review.
- Disbursements must be reconciled against the core banking system every day.
- To save storage, rejected applications should be deleted after 90 days.
- Operations wants reports as needed.
