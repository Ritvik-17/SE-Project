Compliance memo: digital personal loan
From: Compliance and Legal (Bank A)

The following obligations apply to the digital personal-loan journey under the regulator's digital lending directions and our own policies:

1. Before the borrower signs, the app must show a Key Fact Statement with the annual percentage rate (APR), all fees and charges, and the repayment schedule.
2. Loan amounts must be disbursed directly into the borrower's own bank account, never through a third-party or pool account.
3. The borrower must have a cooling-off period of at least 3 days after disbursement in which to exit the loan by repaying the principal and the proportionate APR, without penalty.
4. The app must show the name and contact details of the grievance redressal officer, and complaints must be tracked to resolution.
5. Rejected applicants must be told the principal reasons for rejection in writing within 7 days.
6. Loan files must be retained for 8 years after loan closure; rejected applications must be retained for 2 years from the decision date.
7. Explicit consent must be captured before the credit report is pulled, and the consent record must be kept.
8. Every credit decision, override and change to customer data must leave an audit trail recording who did what and when.
