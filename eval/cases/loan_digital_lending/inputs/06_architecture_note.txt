Architecture note: integrations and non-functional targets
Author: Solution architect (Bank A)

Credit bureau: the credit report is fetched through the bureau's REST API over mutual TLS. The call must complete within 3 seconds at the 95th percentile. If the bureau is unavailable, the application moves to the manual underwriting queue instead of failing.

Core banking: the core banking system is a legacy platform. Loan accounts are created through a nightly batch file.

Disbursement: funds are sent in real time through the payments hub's IMPS API.

Capacity: the platform must support 2,000 concurrent applicants during marketing campaigns.

Availability: 99.9% monthly availability, excluding the planned maintenance window on Sundays from 01:00 to 03:00.
