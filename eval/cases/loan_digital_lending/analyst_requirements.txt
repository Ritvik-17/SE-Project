The system shall let salaried customers apply for a personal loan entirely in a mobile app.
The app shall be available in Hindi and English.
The application process shall be fast and user-friendly.
The system shall show the Key Fact Statement with EMI, APR, all fees and the repayment schedule before the borrower accepts or signs.
Customers shall be able to upload salary slips and bank statements from the phone.
The customer session shall last at least 30 minutes so customers are not logged out while uploading documents.
Customers shall be able to track the status of their application in the app.
Rejected applicants shall be told the principal reasons for rejection in writing within 7 days.
Sanctioned loan amounts shall be disbursed directly to the borrower's own bank account on the same day.
Release 1 shall cover unsecured personal loans to salaried customers from INR 50,000 to INR 10,00,000 with tenures of 12 to 60 months.
The system shall automatically approve loans up to INR 10 lakh without manual review.
Sales agents shall see their pipeline of leads and the status of each application they sourced.
The product shall increase digital personal-loan disbursals by 30% within 12 months of launch.
Release 1 shall go live by 31 March within a budget of INR 2 crore.
The system shall check eligibility: age 21 to 60, at least 12 months of continuous employment and a credit score of at least 700.
The system shall reject applications whose FOIR including the proposed EMI exceeds 50% of net monthly income.
The automated decision engine shall approve only loans up to INR 5 lakh within policy; above INR 5 lakh a credit officer approves and above INR 15 lakh the regional credit head.
Every override shall be recorded with a reason code and approver identity, and the preparer cannot approve the same case (maker-checker).
The system shall detect duplicate applications using the same PAN or device within 30 days and refer them to the fraud team.
The system shall produce a monthly portfolio report with approval rates and 30+ DPD delinquency for the risk committee.
The borrower shall have a cooling-off period of at least 3 days after disbursement to exit by repaying principal and proportionate APR without penalty.
The app shall show the grievance redressal officer's contact details and track complaints to resolution.
Loan files shall be retained for 8 years after closure and rejected applications for 2 years from the decision date.
The system shall capture explicit consent before pulling the credit report and keep the consent record.
Every credit decision, override and change to customer data shall leave an audit trail of who did what and when.
Customers shall sign in with an OTP to the registered mobile number and the app shall be bound to the device.
A step-up OTP shall be required before the customer e-signs the loan agreement.
Customer sessions shall time out after 10 minutes of inactivity.
Staff users shall sign in through single sign-on with multi-factor authentication.
Personal data shall be encrypted at rest and all connections shall use TLS 1.2 or higher.
Security events shall be sent to the SIEM within 5 minutes.
The system must be secure.
The system shall fetch the credit report from the bureau API over mutual TLS within 3 seconds at the 95th percentile.
If the credit bureau is unavailable, the application shall move to the manual underwriting queue.
Loan accounts shall be created in the core banking system through a nightly batch file.
Funds shall be disbursed in real time through the payments hub IMPS API.
The platform shall support 2,000 concurrent applicants.
The platform shall be 99.9% available monthly, excluding maintenance on Sundays 01:00-03:00.
Credit officers shall have a work queue of referred applications, each decided within 4 working hours.
Salary slips and bank statements shall be read by OCR, with manual review when extracted income differs from declared income.
Disbursements shall be reconciled against the core banking system daily.
Rejected applications shall be deleted after 90 days to save storage.
Operations shall get reports as needed.
